How it works

One sentence in. Ready-to-save expenses out.

  1. 1

    Type it or say it

    In your own words, in any order. Speech is recognized on the phone, so no audio ever leaves it.

  2. 2

    Kishenya fills in the rest

    Amount, category, account and date. Short phrases are understood on the phone itself. AI takes the longer ones, several expenses at once.

  3. 3

    You check and save

    Every suggestion comes back as fields you can tap to fix. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Features

Everything you need to see where the money goes.

  • Two taps, even with no signal

    A big keypad for one-handed entry. Everything is saved on your phone first, so adding an expense never waits for the network.

  • Reports that answer the question

    A donut chart by category, the total and the daily average for any period. History grouped by day, with a total for each one.

  • Budgets that warn you early

    Set a monthly limit per category. You get a heads-up at 80% and when it's used up. Nothing else, no marketing.

  • Card and cash, kept apart

    Card, cash and any accounts you add, with real balances. Transfers between them never count as spending.

  • ₴

    Hryvnia, euro, dollar, złoty

    Official National Bank of Ukraine rates, or your own. The rate is saved with each entry, so old reports never change.

  • Your history comes with you

    Back up to a file, for example in iCloud Drive, and restore it any time. Coming from MoneyOK? Import your CSV export.

Private by default

Your money data lives on your phone.

Kishenya works fully offline and needs no account. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

  • Your voice stays on the device

    Speech is turned into text by the phone itself. No audio is sent anywhere.

  • AI only when you ask for it

    Short phrases like “coffee 85” never leave the phone. AI for longer ones needs a free account, and the app asks before it sends a phrase.

  • Leave whenever you like

    Your whole history exports to a file that is yours to keep.

Help

Questions, answered.

Something else? Write to kishenyasupport@gmail.com. Found a bug? In the app, open Settings, then Report a bug.

Is Kishenya free?

Yes. The app is free, with no ads and nothing to buy inside it.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything except AI parsing works without one. To let AI understand longer phrases, sign in with email, Google or Apple. The account is free and covers up to 100 AI requests a day.

What is sent to AI?

Only the phrase you typed or said, together with the names of your categories, accounts and currencies, so the answer fits your setup. It goes to OpenAI, and only after you have agreed. Your saved expenses and balances are not sent.

How do I move to a new phone?

In Settings, open Backup & restore and save a backup file, for example to iCloud Drive. On the new phone, choose Restore from a backup during setup. Accounts, history and settings all come over.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings, tap the account card at the top, then Delete account. The expenses on your phone stay where they are.

Is there an Android or iPad version?

Kishenya is made for iPhone. On an iPad it runs in iPhone mode. There is no Android version yet.
